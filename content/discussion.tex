\section{Discussion}
An additional point is the presence of two candidate reference transitions at 1085.84 keV and 1089.74 keV. Their separation is only approximately 3.90 keV, whereas the fitted energy width of the experimental feature assigned to the latter is approximately 48.57 keV. Consequently, these transitions cannot be resolved as separate peaks in the present measured spectrum using the fitted peak model.

The detection procedure identifies prominent features rather than every possible gamma-ray transition. Consequently, weak transitions and unresolved neighbouring peaks may not appear as separate detected maxima.
