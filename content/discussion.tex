\section{Discussion}
\begin{itemize}
	\item The detection procedure identifies prominent features rather than every possible gamma-ray transition. Weak transitions and closely spaced neighbours therefore remain unresolved or fall below the detection threshold.

	\item  The residuals in \cref{tab:calibration} show a systematic curvature (positive at both ends, negative in the middle), so the linear fit is only approximate. A quadratic term, $E(x) = a + bx + cx^2$, can be added and compared with the linear fit through its residuals and RMSE.

	\item The unresolved structure near 910-930 (\cref{fig:gaussian fit}) can be fitted with a multi-Gaussian model instead of a single Gaussian.

	\item The calibration could be independently checked using additional radioactive sources with well-established gamma-ray energies, such as $^{137}\mathrm{Cs}$ and $^{60}\mathrm{Co}$.

	\item The uncertainties in the fitted peak centroids can be propagated to estimate the uncertainties in the calibration coefficients, providing a more complete assessment of the reliability of the energy calibration.
\end{itemize}
